Built on conversations.
Driven by causes.

Envision was founded on a simple principle: fundraising done right means bringing donors, fundraisers and charities together through equal respect. Through hard work and dedicated teams, we've grown from a startup in Ottawa to an established company with more than 500,000 donors behind us and our sights set on a million. We've battled through everything from bad weather to a pandemic, and now we're crossing borders to bring Envision to a city near you. Every conversation is a chance to inspire change, and we're just getting started.

What we stand for.

The principles behind every conversation, every campaign, and every hire.

Integrity

We do what we say we'll do. Our charity partners trust us with their brand, and we take that responsibility seriously — no exceptions, no shortcuts.

People First

Behind every number is a person — a donor, a fundraiser, a charity worker. We build relationships, not transactions.

Excellence

Every interaction reflects on the causes we represent. Excellence is our aim, whether it's in how we train our teams, reach our donors, or report and coordinate with our partners.

Always Growing

Whether it's growing our teams or growing with the times, we're always looking for ways to improve. We listen, we adapt, and we keep getting better.
