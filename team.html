Meet the Team

The people crazy enough to think fundraising should actually be fun.

John MacInnis

CEO & Founder

Sales showed me there was a world out there ready to give. Charity taught me what they should be giving for. I started Envision with one goal: build a company where donors, fundraisers and charities come together through equal respect to cultivate the change we all know is possible.

“Inspire Change.”

Aidan Hughes

C.O.O.

From setting company records that still stand today to leading Envision's growth with a sharp mind for strategy, Aidan is a paragon of what's possible here. Even stronger than his skill is his support. He'll never miss your call, he'll always have your back, and when you need him, he'll be in your city, right by your side, helping you grow.

“Macro patience, micro speed.”

Oliver Beatty

Director Liaison & Analytics

With over a decade of experience, Oliver has been with us since 2017. From joining a team, to running them, to overseeing company analytics, Oliver has the practical experience from the breadth of positions this industry offers to help guide his work and our teams. Dedicated, honest and always up for a challenge.

“Fear in the face of adversity is fuel for others’ failure.”

Michael Beatty

Payroll & Administration

With us since 2016, Michael began as one of our door-to-door fundraisers. After launching and leading multiple offices over the course of eight years, his broad industry knowledge and attention to detail made him a perfect fit for his current position. Michael believes you cannot effectively oversee or relate to a role if you haven't performed that role yourself, and he is proud to be able to relate to every level of our organization.

“Life is a lot easier if you always do what you say you're going to do.”

Krystal Shannon

Director of Strategic Partnerships

For many at Envision today, and many more to come, Krystal is the first point of contact. She has always believed in the power of genuine relationships, and that belief, together with her passion and work ethic, has helped her grow some of Toronto's most successful teams, and our company as a whole, over her ten years with Envision. Every great team starts with the right first conversation, and more often than not, that conversation is with Krystal.

“You have to act as if it were possible to radically transform the world. And you have to do it all the time.” — Angela Davis
